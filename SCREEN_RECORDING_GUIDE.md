# Screen Recording Guide

1. Start the app with `npm run dev` and open its local URL.
2. Show the home page and scroll through the popular picks and full menu.
3. Demonstrate a category filter, a search, and the empty-results message.
4. Add two different items to the cart; increase and decrease a quantity and show the total update.
5. Remove an item, then show the empty-cart message.
6. Save a menu item to Favorites and open the Favorites page.
7. Optionally submit the pickup form with sample details to show the frontend confirmation. Do not enter real personal information.

The form is a demonstration only. It does not submit data to a server, accept payment, or create a real order.
